Corporate Heritage

Our Story

Est. 2018

How WorkNest Interiors Was Born

“WorkNest Interiors was founded by Anand and Meena after years of dealing with chronic back pain during their corporate careers. After spending nearly ₹80,000 on physiotherapy, they left their IT careers in 2018 to build ergonomic workspace solutions.”

Having experienced first-hand the toll that poorly designed workplace furniture takes on health, focus, and long-term career stamina, our founders dedicated themselves to creating professional furniture solutions that solve posture fatigue permanently. Today, WorkNest Interiors blends engineering rigor with refined corporate aesthetics to serve high-performance teams.

Designing for the human body, not just the physical space.

— Anand & Meena, Founders
